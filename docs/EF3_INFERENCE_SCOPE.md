# EF-3 inference scope (pre-EF-4 audit)

This note fixes the inferential scope of Extended FALI Phase EF-3 before
canonical-template confirmation (EF-4).

## 1. Prompt cohort overlap with historical Phase 1–3

EF-3 uses the **same historical two-template mixed prompt cohorts** as prior
Phase 1 and Phase 3 analyses (seeds 42, 1, 8, 22; 100 template A + 100
template B per seed; N=200 per seed).

Therefore coarse quantities already observed under this distribution are **not
independent replications** in EF-3:

- `conditional_gain`
- aggregate pattern / `Phi_SCORE` (historical P-channel)
- value (`phi_V`)
- context (`phi_C`)

Historical Phase-3 factorial data and the EF-3 historical bridge validate
that Extended FALI recovers these coarse channels on the same prompts.

## 2. What is new in EF-3

Extended FALI's **fine-grained score decomposition** is new relative to
historical P×V×C:

- `phi_T` — target final-query score coordinate
- `phi_R` — rival final-query score coordinates
- `phi_O` — earlier-query score coordinates

EF-3 is confirmatory for hypotheses frozen before EF-2 on coarse/functionally
defined quantities, and exploratory/discovery for T/R/O structure.

## 3. Key EF-3 discovery carried forward

Under the preregistered two-template distribution and discovery rule:

**L10H2:** `phi_T > 0` with `phi_R < 0` (negative rival-score Owen allocation).

**L10H10:** `phi_T > 0` with `phi_R < 0`.

These are **discovery candidates** for EF-4 canonical confirmation, not
established mechanisms.

## 4. Interpretation of `phi_R`

`phi_R` is the **Owen allocation** associated with switching the full set of
competing final-query scores under the specified Extended FALI intervention
game.

It is **not automatically**:

- pure inhibition;
- a direct causal effect in a unique natural pathway;
- a descriptive attention quantity.

It includes interaction allocation under standard Owen Shapley on the score
quotient.

Do not describe `phi_R` as "rival inhibition proven."

## 5. What EF-4 genuinely holds out

Historical canonical Phase-4 data already reveal coarse P/V/C behavior and
functional recruitment effects on the **30-template canonical distribution**.

EF-4 is genuinely held-out **only for new fine-grained T/R/O attributions**
(and their preregistered directional tests if frozen after EF-3 discovery).

Already-observed coarse quantities must not be re-framed as independent EF-4
confirmations.

## 6. Permitted vs prohibited language

**Permitted after EF-3 correction:**

- "EF-3 supports the preregistered two-template hypotheses."
- "Negative rival-score Owen contributions for L10H2 and L10H10 passed the
  preregistered discovery rule."
- "These fine-grained score hypotheses require canonical-template
  confirmation."

**Prohibited:**

- "independently replicated"
- "general mechanism established"
- "rival inhibition proven"
- "Extended FALI validated generally"

Preserve this distinction in any future manuscript.
