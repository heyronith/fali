\caption{\textbf{Target-attention adaptation and positive functional recruitment are distinct in GPT-2 IOI.}
\textbf{(a)} L10H2 and L10H10 both increase attention to the indirect object target after ablation of the canonical primary Name Movers, but their conditional gains have opposite signs: L10H2 becomes more causally useful whereas L10H10 becomes less useful.
\textbf{(b)} Fine FALI attribution shows positive target-score and negative rival-score contributions for both candidates; other-score and value contributions are small, while the surrounding-context contribution differs substantially in magnitude.
\textbf{(c)} The coarse decomposition explains the functional difference: L10H10 retains a positive SCORE contribution, but a much larger negative surrounding-context contribution produces a negative total conditional gain.
Points are exact balanced means and intervals are hierarchical 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals over the canonical template design.
