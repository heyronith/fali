\textbf{(a)} GPT-2 IOI L10H2 and Pythia-160M induction L8H2 both show positive conditional gain after their respective primary interventions.
Separate numerical axes are used because the two experiments use different task metrics and their raw utility magnitudes should not be compared directly.
\textbf{(b)} The observed fine target/rival signs reverse: GPT-2 is target-positive and rival-negative, whereas Pythia is target-negative and rival-positive; their surrounding-context contributions also differ in sign.
The preregistered Pythia test confirms induction behavior, primary-head importance, and positive conditional recruitment (G0--G2), but the predicted target-positive/rival-negative transfer gates G3--G4 are not confirmed, giving an overall EF-6 transfer-gate failure.
This failed transfer shows that a common functional recruitment response need not imply the same fine-grained mechanism in the two studied settings.}
