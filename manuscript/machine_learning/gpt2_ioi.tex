\section{GPT-2 IOI: Conditional Recruitment and Fine-Score Decomposition}\label{sec:gpt2}

\subsection{Experimental design}\label{sec:gpt2-design}

We first apply FALI to GPT-2 Small on indirect object identification (IOI), a setting with a well-characterized attention-head circuit and previously identified Name Mover and Backup Name Mover heads \citep{wang2023ioi}. The perturbed state $B$ zero-ablates the three canonical primary Name Movers L9H6, L9H9, and L10H0 at their full head outputs. The intact state $A$ leaves these heads unmodified. Our task metric is the final-position logit difference between the indirect-object (IO) token and the subject (S) token.

The confirmatory FALI analysis uses 15 low-level IOI templates and both BABA and ABBA prompt structures, giving 30 template--pattern strata. For each of four independently generated prompt cohorts (seeds 42, 1, 8, and 22), we sample 30 prompts per stratum, for $900$ prompts per seed and $3600$ prompts in total. The two primary candidate heads are L10H2 and L10H10. L9H7 and L9H0 serve as architectural controls because the primary intervention occurs at the same layer as those heads: under a full L9 \texttt{hook\_z} intervention, their own cached score and value tensors are unchanged between $A$ and $B$.

This stage is confirmatory specifically for the fine-grained score channels $\phi_T$, $\phi_R$, and $\phi_O$. Coarser pattern/value/context quantities had already been analyzed on these canonical cohorts before the fine FALI hypotheses were frozen, so we do not treat those coarse quantities as held-out confirmation. The preregistered fine-channel hypotheses were $\phi_T>0$ and $\phi_R<0$ for each of L10H2 and L10H10; no directional hypothesis was made for $\phi_O$.

Point estimates equally weight the 30 strata within each seed and then equally weight the four seed cohorts. Uncertainty is estimated with 10,000 deterministic hierarchical template-cluster bootstrap draws: low-level template clusters are sampled with replacement, their paired BABA/ABBA strata are retained, prompts are independently resampled within each seed--template--pattern cell, and the four seed means are then averaged equally. A preregistered strict non-overlap sensitivity excludes low-level template 0, which exactly overlaps a legacy discovery template, leaving 14 template clusters and 28 strata. Thus the main fine-channel conclusions are required to survive both broad-template sampling and removal of the known overlap.

\subsection{Conditional recruitment is not attention increase}\label{sec:gpt2-functional}

Before interpreting FALI channels, we re-establish the functional quantity being explained. In the broad canonical cohort, L10H2 has a positive conditional effect of $0.382$ (95\% hierarchical CI $[0.341,0.424]$) and a positive conditional gain of $0.298$ ($[0.263,0.335]$). It therefore satisfies our definition of positive functional recruitment: after the primary Name Movers are removed, L10H2 remains beneficial and becomes more causally useful.

Target attention alone does not identify this functional response. L10H2 increases attention to the IO target by $0.279$ ($[0.262,0.296]$), but L10H10 shows an even larger target-attention increase of $0.310$ ($[0.285,0.335]$) while its conditional gain is negative, $-0.263$ ($[-0.302,-0.225]$). On this broad canonical distribution, L10H10's conditional effect is also negative, $-0.079$ ($[-0.111,-0.046]$). Thus the larger visible attention adaptation occurs in a head that becomes less useful and, on average over these canonical prompts, harmful after the primary knockout.

This broad-template result also clarifies a distributional qualification from the earlier corrected functional screen. On the initial mixed two-template cohorts, L10H10 remained supportive after the knockout but was downregulated in relative usefulness; on the broader canonical cohort its average conditional effect crosses below zero. The stable conclusion across these analyses is narrower: a strong attention increase is not sufficient evidence of positive functional recruitment. We therefore treat the output-grounded conditional change, rather than attention change, as the FALI estimand.

\subsection{Canonical FALI mechanism}\label{sec:gpt2-fali}

FALI resolves the canonical cross-state changes into score, value, and surrounding-context contributions. For L10H2, the coarse SCORE contribution is positive ($\Phi_{\mathrm{SCORE}}=0.445$) while the surrounding-context contribution is negative ($\phi_C=-0.146$); the value contribution is near zero ($\phi_V=-0.00035$). The fine score decomposition is strongly opposed: the target-score contribution is $\phi_T=0.783$ (95\% CI $[0.722,0.838]$), while the rival-score contribution is $\phi_R=-0.341$ ($[-0.372,-0.311]$). The remaining-score contribution is small and positive ($\phi_O=0.0039$). All four seed means have the preregistered signs, and all 30 template--pattern strata have positive $\phi_T$ and negative $\phi_R$ means.

L10H10 shows the same fine-score sign pattern despite its negative total conditional gain. Its target-score contribution is positive, $\phi_T=0.329$ ($[0.300,0.355]$), and its rival contribution negative, $\phi_R=-0.126$ ($[-0.139,-0.113]$); $\phi_O=0.0036$. At the coarse level, SCORE is positive ($0.206$) but is outweighed by a much larger negative surrounding-context contribution ($\phi_C=-0.471$), yielding the negative endpoint change. This distinction is useful mechanistically: a candidate can exhibit a favorable-looking score adaptation under the specified game without becoming a functionally recruited backup.

The preregistered EF-4 fine-score gate therefore passes for both heads. Each of the four directional hypotheses is confirmed in all four seeds and all 30 canonical strata. The stricter template-0-excluded analysis also retains the same directions: L10H2 has $\phi_T=0.776$ with CI $[0.713,0.836]$ and $\phi_R=-0.337$ with CI $[-0.369,-0.306]$; L10H10 has $\phi_T=0.325$ with CI $[0.294,0.353]$ and $\phi_R=-0.123$ with CI $[-0.136,-0.111]$. The two same-layer controls behave as architectural invariants: across the canonical runs, L9H7 and L9H0 have zero recorded $T$, $R$, $O$, and $V$ attributions, with their endpoint changes allocated to $C$ up to numerical residual $1.11\times10^{-16}$.

These results do not imply that positive target and negative rival contributions constitute a universal recruitment mechanism. First, L10H10 displays the same fine-score signs without positive recruitment. Second, the FALI values allocate interactions under the specified intervention game rather than identify pure direct effects. We therefore treat the GPT-2 result as a well-supported mechanism pattern in this setting and test its transfer separately in Section~\ref{sec:pythia}.

\subsection{Intervention robustness}\label{sec:gpt2-robustness}

We next asked whether the four preregistered GPT-2 fine-channel signs depend on the primary-damage intervention. In addition to the canonical zero-ablation analysis, EF-5 evaluated matched-mean and matched-resample primary-damage schemes on the same broad template design. Under matched-mean damage, all four channels retained their original directions with hierarchical CIs excluding zero: L10H2 had $\phi_T=0.440$ ($[0.408,0.470]$) and $\phi_R=-0.166$ ($[-0.178,-0.152]$), while L10H10 had $\phi_T=0.188$ ($[0.173,0.202]$) and $\phi_R=-0.0816$ ($[-0.0885,-0.0744]$). Under matched-resample damage, the same signs remained: L10H2 $\phi_T=0.265$ ($[0.245,0.285]$), $\phi_R=-0.0474$ ($[-0.0572,-0.0380]$); L10H10 $\phi_T=0.0858$ ($[0.0776,0.0941]$), $\phi_R=-0.0150$ ($[-0.0189,-0.0114]$). Each tested alternative was classified \textsc{Robust} by the preregistered channel criteria, with the predicted direction in all 30 strata.

However, the preregistered EF-5 experiment-level gate did \emph{not} pass. Its reference-perturbation criterion required the zero-ablation damage-drop hierarchical confidence interval to lie entirely above zero; the observed zero-reference quantity was $-0.319$ with CI $[-0.396,-0.244]$, so that assumption failed and triggered the preregistered stop condition. We therefore make the bounded robustness claim supported by the data: the target-positive/rival-negative fine-channel signs persist under the matched-mean and matched-resample alternatives that were tested. We do not claim robustness to arbitrary intervention choices, and we report the failed overall EF-5 gate rather than treating the successful channel checks as a global pass.
